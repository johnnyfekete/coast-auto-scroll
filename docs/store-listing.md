# Chrome Web Store listing

Draft copy. The permission model is the lead, not a footnote — no competitor in
this category can make the same claim, and it is the one thing a reader can
verify for themselves in `chrome://extensions`.

## Name

**Coast — Auto Scroll for Reading**

Keyword first would rank marginally better on the store's own search; the brand
first is what makes the extension recommendable out loud and gives the
repository and any future site something to be. The keyword is still in the
title either way.

## Short description (132 characters max)

> Scrolls the page for you at a speed you set. Installs with no permissions —
> pin a site and it asks for that one site.

## Full description

> **Coast scrolls the page for you, so you can read without a hand on the
> wheel.** Long articles, documentation, recipes on a kitchen counter, guitar
> tabs with both hands busy.
>
> **It installs asking for nothing.**
>
> Most auto-scroll extensions want to "read and change all your data on all
> websites" before you have scrolled a single page. Coast declares no site
> access at all. From the toolbar it uses the one tab you clicked on. Pin a site
> and Chrome asks about that site, by name, once — and unpinning gives the
> access back. Open chrome://extensions and Coast will say "On specific sites",
> listing exactly the ones you chose.
>
> **What it does**
>
> • Start and stop from the toolbar on any page
> • Pin the sites you read often, and a small control sits in their corner —
>   drag it wherever it is out of the way
> • Scroll it yourself and it pauses, then carries on from where you left it
> • Keeps up with infinite feeds, and stops when an article has actually ended
> • Each pinned site remembers its own speed
> • A keyboard shortcut, with nothing bound to it until you pick a key
> • Light and dark, following your system
>
> **No account. No telemetry. No network requests of any kind.** Your settings
> stay on your machine — not even Chrome sync, because the list of sites you
> read is not something Coast should send anywhere.
>
> Open source, GPL-3.0: github.com/johnnyfekete/coast-auto-scroll

## Search terms the listing should carry naturally

auto scroll, autoscroll, scroll automatically, hands-free reading, page
scroller, reading mode, teleprompter-style scrolling, guitar tab scroll,
scroll speed control.

Not stuffed into the title: Chrome's own guidance treats keyword-stuffed titles
as a policy problem, and the incumbents named literally "AutoScroll" already own
that exact match with hundreds of thousands of installs. There is no winning it
by name collision.
